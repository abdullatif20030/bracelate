Since 2016

Made slowly, on purpose.

AUREL began on a workbench in Lisbon, where founder Inês Duarte was trained in traditional goldsmithing before deciding the trade needed fewer, better pieces rather than more of them.

Every chain is still cut, formed, and soldered by hand. We work almost entirely in recycled precious metals, and we test every clasp a hundred times before it reaches a wrist.
